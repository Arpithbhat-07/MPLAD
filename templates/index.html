About MPLADS AI Risk Management System

This system assists district authorities, monitoring committees, and auditors in identifying potential anomalies in Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS) fund execution.

Responsible AI Disclosure

All AI flags indicate potential irregularities and deviations from standard timelines or financial benchmarks. They do not constitute a legal or final determination of fraud and require on-site technical verification by authorized officers.

Risk Categories Explained:

  • High Risk: Significant variance between fund disbursements and physical progress, or milestone delays exceeding acceptable limits. Requires immediate physical verification.
  • Medium Risk: Moderate variances or single-factor anomalies requiring desktop audit before releasing next fund tranche.
  • Low Risk: Normal progress within anticipated parameters. Routine quarterly monitoring.
